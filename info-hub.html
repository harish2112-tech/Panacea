Myth vs Fact

Don't fall for misinformation. Here are the facts:

MYTH

"Vaccines contain microchips"

FACT

Vaccines contain only mRNA, lipids, salts, and sugars. They do not contain any tracking devices or microchips.

MYTH

"Young people don't get seriously ill"

FACT

While older adults face higher risks, young people can also get severely ill and experience long-term complications.

MYTH

"Natural immunity is better than vaccines"

FACT

Vaccine immunity is safer and more predictable. Natural infection carries risks of severe illness, death, and long-term effects.
